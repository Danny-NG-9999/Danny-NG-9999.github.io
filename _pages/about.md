## 🚀 About Me
I'm an MSc Financial Economics graduate from the University of Exeter with professional experience in Transaction Advisory at Grant Thornton and Technology Risk Consulting at KPMG. My background sits at the intersection of finance, risk, and data analytics, where I've helped organisations evaluate investments, identify business risks, strengthen governance, and support strategic decision-making.

Professionally, at Grant Thornton, I supported buy-side and sell-side M&A transactions by performing financial due diligence, assessing earnings quality, evaluating revenue sustainability, and analysing financial, contractual, and operational risks. My work uncovered significant EBITDA adjustments, unrecorded liabilities, and customer concentration risks, helping clients strengthen negotiations, protect deal value, and make more informed investment decisions. Previously at KPMG, I worked on IT risk assurance and data governance engagements, assessing control environments, validating data integrity, and supporting regulatory compliance across complex enterprise systems.

Alongside my professional experience, I have developed a portfolio of analytics projects to strengthen my technical expertise and apply quantitative methods to real-world business challenges. These include building an end-to-end Business Intelligence platform using SQL, Python, and Power BI, developing a Basel IRB-aligned credit risk modelling framework (PD, LGD, EAD, and Expected Loss), and conducting econometric research using Panel Vector Autoregression (PVAR) to investigate the relationship between airport activity and regional productivity.

Highlights:
- **💼 Transaction Advisory:** Identified a £420k EBITDA adjustment and ~£500k in unrecorded liabilities, strengthening valuation analysis and supporting better-informed acquisition decisions.
- **📊 Business Intelligence:** Designed an end-to-end analytics solution integrating 180,000+ operational records into executive dashboards with interactive KPIs and self-service reporting.
- **💳 Credit Risk Analytics:** Built interpretable PD, LGD, EAD, and Expected Loss models using 250,000+ consumer loans, supporting credit underwriting, stress testing, and risk-based pricing.
- **📈 Quantitative Research:** Applied Panel VAR, Granger causality, and advanced econometric techniques to evaluate regional productivity drivers and produce evidence-based policy recommendations.
- **🏆 Recognition:** Recipient of the Exeter Award, contributor to a high-impact sustainability consultancy project, and ranked Top 15 of 164 teams in the VOIZ Climate Simulation Challenge.

What motivates me most is using data to uncover insights that influence decisions. Whether evaluating investment opportunities, modelling financial risk, or designing executive dashboards, I enjoy transforming complex data into clear, actionable recommendations that help organisations improve performance, manage uncertainty, and make confident, evidence-based decisions.

I'm currently seeking opportunities in Risk Analytics, Financial Analytics, and Data Analytics, where I can combine analytical thinking, commercial awareness, and technical expertise to create meaningful business impact.

In my personal time, I enjoy exploring local cafés, attending community events, and spending time in green spaces. These activities allow me to connect with others, appreciate nature, and maintain a balanced lifestyle.
