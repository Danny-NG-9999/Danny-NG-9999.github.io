## 🎯 Objective
Developed an end-to-end Business Intelligence solution for **DataCo Global** that transformed fragmented sales, logistics, customer, and web traffic data into a unified reporting platform. Built an interactive executive dashboard analysing over **180,000 records**, enabling stakeholders to monitor business performance, identify operational inefficiencies, optimise pricing and logistics, and make faster, data-driven decisions across the organisation.

---

## ⚙️ Tasks

### **Data Engineering & ETL**
* Built an end-to-end ETL pipeline using **Python** to clean, transform, validate, and integrate over **180,000 records** from multiple business domains.
* Designed a **Fact Constellation (Galaxy) Schema** with **2 Fact** and **8 Dimension** tables, creating a scalable data model that supports cross-functional reporting and analysis.

### **Database & Data Modelling**
* Developed a relational **MySQL** database with primary keys, foreign keys, and data integrity constraints to support efficient analytical queries.
* Created and validated an **Entity Relationship Diagram (ERD)** to ensure accurate relationships between sales, customer, product, logistics, and web data.

### **Business Intelligence & Analytics**
* Built an interactive **Power BI** dashboard using **DAX**, **Power Query**, **Deneb**, and geospatial visualisations to deliver drill-through analysis, dynamic KPIs, forecasting, and customer segmentation.
* Analysed sales performance, profitability, discount strategies, web conversion, and shipping operations to identify business challenges and opportunities for improvement.

---

## 💡 Results

### **Operational Insights**
* Identified that **54.8% of orders were delivered late**, revealing fulfilment inefficiencies that reduced profitability and customer satisfaction.
* Showed that delivery performance was driven by operational processes rather than order volume, highlighting opportunities to improve logistics efficiency.

### **Commercial Performance**
* Discovered that the web channel generated only **3.04% of total revenue**, while customer conversion increased to **22.94% on Thursdays (2.5× higher)**, providing clear opportunities to improve digital marketing and online sales.
* Identified profit margin erosion caused by excessive discounting, supporting more targeted pricing and promotional strategies.

### **Decision Support**
* Delivered an executive dashboard with **15+ interactive KPIs**, enabling business users to monitor performance, investigate root causes, and explore trends through self-service analytics.
* Translated complex operational data into practical recommendations across pricing, logistics, customer experience, and digital commerce to support strategic decision-making.
